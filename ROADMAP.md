# COINPILOT AI — Roadmap

## v1.5 — Current baseline
- Market Pulse holdings highlight and exit alerts.
- Paper BUY workflow and staged R-multiple exits.
- Public Binance Spot market-data scanner.

## v1.6 — Daily Trade Ledger
- One parent trade record per position.
- Each partial exit stored as a linked execution event.
- Correct realised P&L after entry/exit fees.
- Daily opening/closing virtual equity and daily net P&L.
- CSV and Excel exports.
- Reconciliation tests to prevent double-counting partial exits.

## Validation gates
- Unit tests for position sizing, staged exits, fees and P&L.
- Historical backtests with realistic fee/slippage assumptions.
- Out-of-sample and walk-forward validation.
- Paper-trading observation period before any discussion of live execution.

No win-rate or profit target is promised. A strategy is only considered promising after repeatable, independently checked results.
