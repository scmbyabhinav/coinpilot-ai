# COINPILOT AI — 50-step product and business roadmap
Master Owner Edition | Product strategy, not a promise of trading returns

## Phase A — Reliability and trading research (steps 1–10)
1. Freeze the current paper-only release as a reproducible baseline.
2. Add a persistent signal event log with deduplication.
3. Add closed-candle-only signal evaluation.
4. Add a configurable BUY alert threshold.
5. Add per-symbol cooldown based on actual trades.
6. Add daily loss and maximum-position circuit breakers.
7. Add paper-trade fee and slippage assumptions.
8. Add complete trade lifecycle audit records.
9. Add signal-history CSV export.
10. Add a repeatable smoke-test checklist for each release.

## Phase B — Strategy validation (steps 11–20)
11. Add multiple independently selectable strategies.
12. Add benchmark buy-and-hold comparisons.
13. Add train/validation/test time splits.
14. Add walk-forward testing.
15. Add out-of-sample performance reports.
16. Add max drawdown and recovery-time metrics.
17. Add expectancy, profit factor and average win/loss.
18. Add sensitivity analysis for fees and slippage.
19. Add regime analysis (trend, range, high volatility).
20. Require a minimum sample and stability gate before labelling a strategy validated.

## Phase C — Product experience (steps 21–30)
21. Improve the dashboard information hierarchy.
22. Add persistent background market-data service.
23. Add desktop notifications with quiet hours.
24. Add configurable watchlists and market filters.
25. Add signal explanations and invalidation conditions.
26. Add portfolio exposure and correlation view.
27. Add risk-per-trade sizing calculator.
28. Add paper-account reset, backup and restore.
29. Add user-exportable monthly performance reports.
30. Add application health, connection and data-freshness monitoring.

## Phase D — Commercial SaaS foundations (steps 31–40)
31. Define the customer personas and product promise.
32. Conduct competitor and willingness-to-pay research.
33. Create Free, Plus and Pro feature boundaries.
34. Keep the founder/owner account permanently free at the highest product tier.
35. Add secure authentication and account recovery.
36. Add server-side subscription entitlements.
37. Add payment-provider integration after legal and tax review.
38. Add customer support, refund and cancellation workflows.
39. Add privacy policy, terms, risk disclosures and data-retention controls.
40. Add admin analytics for activation, retention, churn and support.

## Phase E — Responsible launch and scale (steps 41–50)
41. Obtain India-specific legal advice on crypto software, paid signals and financial-advice boundaries.
42. Review applicable FIU, VDA, advertising, consumer and tax obligations with qualified advisers.
43. Create a documented signal-performance methodology.
44. Publish independently reproducible historical and paper results, including losing periods.
45. Run a closed beta with informed users and no return promises.
46. Add abuse prevention, rate limits and account security monitoring.
47. Complete security review and dependency vulnerability checks.
48. Add a signed Windows installer and automatic updates.
49. Launch a small paid pilot only after compliance and support readiness.
50. Scale only if retention, customer outcomes, reliability and unit economics justify it.

## Proposed commercial model (hypotheses to validate)
- Free: delayed/basic scanner, limited watchlist and education.
- Plus: richer charts, alerts and research tools.
- Pro: advanced analytics, strategy lab and exportable reports.
- Founder/Owner: lifetime highest-tier access at no charge.

Illustrative test pricing only: ₹299/month Plus and ₹799/month Pro. Validate willingness to pay before setting prices. Do not sell guaranteed returns, fabricated win rates, or unverified BUY/SELL calls. Paid signals and personalized recommendations require specific legal review before launch.

## Non-negotiable guardrails
- Paper trading first; no live order execution in this roadmap's initial releases.
- No withdrawal permission on any exchange API key.
- Never claim a strategy is profitable without reproducible, cost-adjusted, out-of-sample evidence.
- Do not use customer funds or custody assets.
- Do not promise income or guaranteed returns.
