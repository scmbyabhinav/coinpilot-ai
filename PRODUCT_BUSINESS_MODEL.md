# COINPILOT AI — Business model

## Product positioning
A crypto market research and trading-workflow platform for self-directed users. It scans public exchange data, explains technical setups, supports paper trading and provides strategy analytics.

## What customers pay for
Customers pay for software utility: market scanning, watchlists, alerts, paper trading, performance analytics, exports, research tools and workflow convenience. They do not pay for promised profits.

## Suggested tiers (pricing hypotheses)
- Free: starter watchlist, basic signals and paper wallet.
- Plus: expanded watchlists, alert history and portfolio analytics.
- Pro: strategy lab, deeper reports, multi-watchlist research and exports.
- Master Owner: founder account, all product features free.

Illustrative prices to test: ₹299/month Plus; ₹799/month Pro. These are not market-validated prices.

## Revenue equation
Monthly recurring revenue = paying subscribers × average monthly subscription revenue.
Example scenarios are planning arithmetic, not forecasts:
- 100 subscribers × ₹299 = ₹29,900/month gross.
- 500 subscribers × ₹499 blended ARPU = ₹2,49,500/month gross.
- 1,000 subscribers × ₹699 blended ARPU = ₹6,99,000/month gross.
Gross revenue excludes payment fees, GST/tax, hosting, support, refunds, marketing and compliance costs. No subscriber or revenue outcome is guaranteed.

## Go-to-market
1. Prove product reliability with a private paper-trading beta.
2. Publish transparent performance methodology and full-period results.
3. Offer free educational content and product demonstrations.
4. Convert users through product utility, not income claims.
5. Track activation, 30/90-day retention, churn, support load and gross margin.
6. Launch paid access only after legal, privacy, tax and consumer compliance review.

## Compliance gate
Before distributing paid crypto signals or personalized recommendations in India, obtain qualified legal advice on applicable financial-advice/securities boundaries, VDA/FIU obligations, advertising, consumer protection, privacy, GST and tax. Do not imply that a software subscription is a regulatory exemption.
